# Zambia Data Protection Act 2021 — Reference

**Status:** Draft | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** As needed

## Overview

Zambia Data Protection Act 2021 provides the legal framework for the processing of personal data in Zambia. This reference summarises the applicable obligations for the founder's AI enterprise, which operates primarily in Zambia.

## Key principles

1. **Lawful, fair, and transparent processing** — personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner.
2. **Purpose limitation** — data collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
3. **Data minimisation** — adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary.
4. **Accuracy** — kept up to date; inaccurate data erased or rectified without delay.
5. **Storage limitation** — kept in identifiable form for no longer than necessary.
6. **Integrity and confidentiality** — processed in a manner ensuring appropriate security.
7. **Accountability** — the controller is responsible for compliance and can demonstrate it.

## Applicability to the enterprise

- The enterprise processes personal data of clients, staff, users, and leads.
- Compliance with Zambia DPA 2021 is mandatory for all Zambian client engagements.
- International data transfer provisions apply where data leaves Zambia.

## Compliance actions

- Register as a data controller with the relevant Zambian authority.
- Appoint a data protection officer where required.
- Conduct data protection impact assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing (e.g., AI customer service, lead qualification).
- Maintain records of processing activities.
