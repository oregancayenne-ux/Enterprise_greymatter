# IP and IP Protection

**Status:** Draft | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Annual

## Purpose

Protects intellectual property created by and for the enterprise, and defines ownership of IP arising from client engagements.

## IP created by the enterprise

| Type | Owner | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Software/code (Hermes, OC8, agents) | Founder | Owned by the enterprise unless assigned |
| Documentation | Founder | Owned by the enterprise |
| Trademarks | Founder | To be registered; `blownapp.io`, `BLOWN` trade dress |
| Designs, content, media | Founder | Owned by the enterprise |

## IP arising from client engagements

- Smart contracts and agreements with clients specify IP ownership of deliverables.
- Client-provided materials remain the client's property unless assigned in writing.
- Open-source components are used under their licences; compliance tracked in `Professional_Advice_Register.md`.

## IP protection measures

1. Source-code and document access restricted to authorised personnel.
2. Trademarks applied for where commercially valuable.
3. Domain registrations held by the founder (not agents).
4. Contracts include confidentiality, IP assignment, and non-compete clauses where appropriate.
