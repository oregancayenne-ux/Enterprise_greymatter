# Contracts

## Purpose

Contract templates for the founder's AI enterprise. Use these as starting points and adapt with legal advice.

## Templates

| Template | Use |
|----------|-----|
| `Contract_Template_Client_Services.md` | Managed AI services to client |
| `Contract_Template_Client_Asset_Management.md` | Asset management / trading services |
| `Contract_Template_NDA.md` | Non-disclosure agreements |

## Notes

- All contracts should be reviewed by legal counsel (`Professional_Advice_Register.md`) before signing.
- Confidentiality, IP assignment, and payment terms must be tailored per engagement.
- Domain and asset ownership clauses must reference the assets in the `Entity_and_Ownership_Register.md`.
