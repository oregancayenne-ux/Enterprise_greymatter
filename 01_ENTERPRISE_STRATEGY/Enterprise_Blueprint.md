# Enterprise Blueprint

**Status:** v1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Annual

## Overview

A flexible direction, not a legal entity chart. Defines the architectural intent of the enterprise: an AI-native technology and venture-building business with a governance-control-execution stack.

## Executive architecture (Rev 1.1, Amendment 2)

- **Founder:** ultimate strategic authority.
- **OC8 (proposed):** executive governance and control platform. Evaluation only; see `../05_PROJECTS/002_OC8_GOVERNANCE_ASSESSMENT/`.
- **Hermes (proposed):** managed execution engine. Assessment; see `../05_PROJECTS/001_HERMES_ENGINE/`.
- **Specialist agents:** departmental and technical workers.
- **Model providers:** interchangeable intelligence resources.
- **Domains:** 810wn.com (corporate); **blownapp.io** (applications — registered and verified).

OC8/Hermes compatibility must be established via proof of concept before production adoption.

## Security architecture (Rev 1.1, Amendment 3)

Four domains with 12 mandatory design principles, consolidated in `../03_SHARED_OS/02_SECURITY/Security_Constitution.md` and `../03_SHARED_OS/02_SECURITY/Zero_Trust_Doctrine.md`. Acceptance gates defined in `Human_Approval_Policy.md`.

## Business model

To be confirmed by the founder. Options under consideration: managed AI services, SaaS platforms, content/digital products, and third-party technology consulting.

## Divisions

- **Client delivery:** commercial engagements (e.g., Pilot Client Automotive).
- **Platform:** internal AI/agent stack (Hermes, OC8).
- **Security:** dedicated cybersecurity service division (Rev 1.1, Amendment 5) — under evaluation.
- **Enterprise / founder office:** the founder's own AI context and tooling.

## Technology architecture (Rev 1.1, Amendment 2)

Model providers must be interchangeable. Technology architecture details are in `../03_SHARED_OS/03_TECHNOLOGY/Technology_Architecture.md`.

## Growth expectations

Aspirational targets (not forecasts): 3–5 paying managed-AI clients in 12–24 months; 1 profitable digital asset; regional recognition in 2–3 years (see `Vision_Mission_Ambition.md`).
