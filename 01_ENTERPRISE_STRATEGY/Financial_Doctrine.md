# Financial Doctrine

**Status:** v1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Quarterly

## Recurring technology budget

USD 50–100/month for all recurring technology expenditure (VPS, AI model usage, utilities, tooling). This is a standing constraint unless the founder explicitly revises it.

## Budget protocol

- Recurring spend above USD 100/month requires founder approval.
- One-time capital expenditure up to USD 500 is at founder discretion.
- Expenditure above USD 500 requires a Decision Register entry.

## Consolidation note

Recorded as ongoing (not "first three months" — the "first three months" wording in earlier drafts is superseded). The outdated phrase, if any, is removed from the canonical text.

## Treasury

- Bank accounts, domain registrations, and legal entity ownership are recorded in `../06_LEGAL_AND_COMPLIANCE/Entity_and_Ownership_Register.md`.
- Receipts and invoices are stored in `../inbox/` until filed under the relevant budget category.
