# Decision Register

**Status:** v1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** As decisions are made

## Purpose

A living register of the enterprise's significant decisions. Each entry records the decision, the context, the rationale, and the outcome. Proposed changes to this register are subject to founder approval.

## Convention

- Decisions are numbered D001, D002, etc., in chronological order.
- Each entry is revised as the situation changes.
- Proposed decisions are marked `(proposed)`; approved decisions are marked `(approved)`.
- Budget figures are in USD unless stated otherwise.

## Decision D001 — Recurring technology budget

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Establish a recurring technology budget of USD 50–100/month.
- **Status:** Approved
- **Rationale:** Cost discipline; preserve capacity for experimental AI tooling.

## Decision D002 — Executive architecture

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Establish OC8 (governance) and Hermes (execution) as the target architecture, subject to proof-of-concept.
- **Status:** Approved (proposed)
- **Rationale:** Per Rev 1.1, Amendment 2.

## Decision D003 — Model provider strategy

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Model providers are interchangeable; prefer multimodal-capable providers with strong security posture.
- **Status:** Approved
- **Rationale:** Vendor flexibility and risk mitigation.

## Decision D004 — Cybersecurity Service Division

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Establish a dedicated cybersecurity service division; evaluate market need and build-out.
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Rationale:** Rev 1.1, Amendment 5 — new revenue line requiring security expertise.

## Decision D005 — Monarx (security tooling)

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Evaluate Monarx as an add-on security product; do not purchase until proof of value.
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Rationale:** Rev 1.1, Amendment 4.

## Decision D006 — Security acceptance gates

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Implement a 10-point security acceptance gate for production deployments.
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Rationale:** Rev 1.1, Amendment 6.

## Decision D007 — Change management

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Treat all constitution and doctrine changes as proposals until founder approval; record in this register.
- **Status:** Approved
- **Rationale:** Rev 1.1, Amendment 7.
