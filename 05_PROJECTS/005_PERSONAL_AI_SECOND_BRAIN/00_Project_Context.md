# Personal AI Second Brain — Project Context

**Status:** Assessment | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder

## Overview

The founder's personal AI context workspace — isolated, access-controlled, and separate from enterprise knowledge.

## Purpose

Store personal AI context, notes, and assets that must remain segregated from client and enterprise data.

Restricted to the founder. Personal contact details, private credentials, and personal health or family information do not belong in the enterprise repository.

## Standards

- Personal assets register in `Personal_Assets_Register.md`.
- All personal data isolated; no cross-linking to enterprise secrets.
