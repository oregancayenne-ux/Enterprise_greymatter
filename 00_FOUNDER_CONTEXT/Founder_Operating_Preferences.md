# Founder Operating Preferences

**Status:** v1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Annual

## Preferences

- **Communication style:** Short, direct answers (default; per-session override).
- **Multi-model consultation:** Flag when a multi-model cross-check would materially improve a decision; on request, restate the prompt pack for other models.
- **Output mode:** Use Standard or Light mode as appropriate to the task (see `AI_Onboarding_Context.md`).
- **Constitution compliance:** Follow the Master Constitution; escalate conflicts to the founder.

## Document hygiene

- Use compact headers: `Title | Status | Version | Date | Owner | Review date`.
- Cross-reference using relative paths.
- Keep the Decision Register current; record changes there.

## Non-negotiables

1. Security and privacy by design.
2. Human approval for consequential actions.
3. Evidence before claims.
4. Cost discipline (budget USD 50–100/month).
5. No irreversible decisions without founder approval.
