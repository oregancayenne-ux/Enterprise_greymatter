# Vision, Mission & Ambition

**Status:** v1.1 (ratified) | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Annual

Integrates Rev 1.1 amendments into the enterprise's strategic direction.

## Mission

Create, operate, improve, acquire (opportunistically, after foundational build), and scale useful, secure, AI-native systems and businesses that generate measurable economic value for clients and communities.

## Vision

To be the most trusted AI-native enterprise in Southern Africa — recognised by clients, partners, and regulators for building secure, useful, and scalable AI systems from a Zambian base and expanding globally.

## Principles

- Security is a commercial capability, not just an operational expense (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
- Start from Zambia; expand globally (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
- Human accountability for consequential decisions.
- Modular, replaceable components; interchangeable model providers.
- Comply with Zambia Data Protection Act 2021 for Zambian client engagements (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
