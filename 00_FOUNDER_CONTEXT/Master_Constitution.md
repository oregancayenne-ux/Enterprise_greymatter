## Founder authority

Founder retains final authority over strategy, constitutional changes, budgets, sensitive data policies, production deployment, and high-impact actions unless formally delegated. Specific bounded delegations (spend caps, reversible actions, drafting, and test execution) are defined in `../02_GOVERNANCE/Human_Approval_Policy.md`.

## Executive architecture

The intended executive architecture (per Rev 1.1, Amendment 2):

- **Founder:** ultimate strategic authority.
- **OC8 (proposed):** executive governance and control platform.
- **Hermes (proposed):** managed execution engine operating under delegated policies.
- **Specialist agents:** departmental and technical workers.
- **Model providers:** interchangeable intelligence resources.

OC8/Hermes compatibility must be established via proof of concept before production adoption. Governance capability is not equivalent to security; neither platform is assumed to be a security boundary on its own. See `../05_PROJECTS/002_OC8_GOVERNANCE_ASSESSMENT/00_Project_Context.md` and `../05_PROJECTS/001_HERMES_ENGINE/00_Project_Context.md`.

## Operating principles

1. Security and privacy by design.
2. Human approval for consequential actions.
3. Evidence before claims; verify current facts and technical compatibility.
4. Cost discipline; preserve the current recurring technology budget of USD 50–100/month unless the founder explicitly revises it.
5. Prefer modular and replaceable components.
6. AI should recommend, research, draft, test, and execute bounded tasks — not silently make irreversible decisions.
7. Client, enterprise, project, and personal knowledge must remain appropriately segregated.
8. No production deployment based solely on a model's recommendation; test and review.
9. Maintain decision records and explain changes.
10. Security is a foundational, non-negotiable principle (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
11. Security is a commercial capability, not merely an operational overhead (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
12. Treat compliance as a business enabler: named legal frameworks (Zambia Data Protection Act 2021) define the baseline for Zambian client engagements (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).

## Jurisdiction & interpretation

1. Governing law for all Zambian client engagements is Zambia law, subject to legal advice.
2. Where canonical documents conflict, the parent doctrine prevails; approved project-level exceptions must be noted in that project's context.
3. Amendments require founder approval; the Decision Register records all proposed changes.
