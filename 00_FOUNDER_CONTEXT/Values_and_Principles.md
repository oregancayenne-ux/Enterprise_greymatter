# Values and Principles

**Status:** v1.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-18 | **Owner:** Founder | **Review:** Annual

## Principles

1. Security and privacy by design.
2. Security is a commercial capability, not merely an operational expense.
3. Human approval for consequential actions — AI supports, never silently decides.
4. Evidence before claims; verify facts and technical compatibility.
5. Cost discipline; preserve recurring technology budget of USD 50–100/month.
6. Prefer modular, replaceable components.
7. Respect clients, staff, users, law, and IP.
8. Comply with Zambia Data Protection Act 2021 for Zambian client engagements (Rev 1.1, Amendment 1).
9. Maintain decision records and explain changes.
10. Security acceptance is a business enabler, not a blocker.
